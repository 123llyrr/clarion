The upstream shader credits https://github.com/4v3ngR/kwin-effects-glass.

Adapted portions: rounded-rectangle signed distance and the finite-difference
normal used to refract the live background. Realand implementation changes:
logical-pixel bounded inward displacement, stationary edge light, one texture
sample without chromatic dispersion, shared GLSL math for GLES/Vulkan,
disconnected protocol-region crops, and conservative fragment-count fallback.
This is an open-source approximation, not Apple's implementation.

Other evaluated projects (no source copied):
https://github.com/OverShifted/LiquidGlass
https://github.com/whynotmake-it/flutter_liquid_glass
The Flutter shader works inside a Flutter scene, whereas Realand must sample
other Wayland clients in the compositor. No upstream installation script ran.
